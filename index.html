📖 Automated Logistics: Agent-Based Modeling Formulation

1. Theoretical Foundation

Designed and built for Atif Ali Shah's university Agent-Based Modeling course, this simulation models the autonomous decision-making and spatial coordination of Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) in an automated fulfillment center.

2. Task Allocation Strategies

  • Strategy A (Nearest Robot): Greedy allocation based purely on Euclidean travel distance to package pickup location.
  • Strategy B (Priority & Battery Aware): Multi-criteria decision function balancing remaining battery capacity, priority order weight (Express vs Standard), and distance.

3. Collision Avoidance & Route Recovery

AMRs execute decentralized proximity radar checks to prevent collisions at intersections. When an aisle is obstructed, robots recalculate alternative routes dynamically using A* search.

⚗️ Logistics Strategy Comparison Laboratory

Run parallel simulations over 1,500 ticks under identical random seeds to empirically benchmark task allocation strategies and fleet sizing.
